Welcome to One Wish Project

At One Wish Project, we believe that every child deserves a memorable birthday celebration, regardless of their circumstances. Our mission is to bring joy and happiness to children facing challenging situations by organizing unforgettable birthday parties and granting their heartfelt wishes. Through the generosity of our supporters and volunteers, we create magical experiences filled with laughter, love, and cherished memories. Join us in making a difference in the lives of these incredible children as we celebrate their special day and deliver hope for a brighter future.

Do we look familiar? Might have seen us on TV before.

Our humble Beginnings

April 29, 2017 at the Lazarus House With her babysitting money, Hannah purchased what she needed to bake her very first birthday cake and purchase presents for 9-year old Abrian. Since then, Hannah has baked over 200 cakes for very deserving children.

Our journey so far

June 3, 2022 at the Lazarus House What a difference 5 years makes. Since that first partnerhip with the Lazarus House in 2017, One Wish Project has expanded to seven additional shelters and the Department of Children and Families.
